# 08 Whitepaper Framing

## Working Title

**Proof Continuity Infrastructure: Keeping Digital Receipts Alive Across Cryptographic Transitions**

## Core Thesis

Most governance and audit systems are passive. They record what happened under today’s mathematics.  
When the mathematics changes (quantum or otherwise), those records can become unverifiable.

VenturePilot treats continuity itself as infrastructure.

By combining continuous discovery (Omni Discovery) with a cryptographic continuity layer (Proof Continuity), organizations can issue receipts that remain valid for decades — even as the underlying algorithms are retired and replaced.

## Key Claims

1. A receipt that cannot survive an algorithm transition is not a long-term record.
2. Continuity must be explicitly re-anchored; it cannot be assumed.
3. Migration Receipts are first-class objects, not afterthoughts.
4. Executives need a single place to see continuity posture the same way they see risk posture.

## Audience

CISOs, Heads of Compliance, IP counsel, enterprise architects, and boards that must answer:  
“Will our most important digital evidence still be provable in 15 years?”
